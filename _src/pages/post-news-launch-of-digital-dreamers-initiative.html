Launch of Digital Dreamers Initiative

February 15, 2023 News

Digital Dreamers is a Vidoori initiative aimed at empowering school-age students towards innovative thought, use of technology, and having the tools needed to take advantage of their education. This initiative is designed to help young people achieve their dreams by providing them with access to the resources needed in today’s digital world. As part of this initiative, support is also offered to the educators responsible for guiding the education process.

The initial efforts of the program are designed to provide technology and educational support to underserved communities, focusing on children who may not have access to technology equipment, software, training, and out-of-the-classroom experiences to help them develop the skills they need to succeed in school and beyond. In addition to technical support, the initiative provides educational support to help children succeed in school. This includes providing educational resources to help children improve their academic performance and reach their full potential.

The five pillars of the initiative are:

  1. Technology Access
  2. Technology Education
  3. Educator Empowerment
  4. Out of the Classroom Education
  5. Nutrition & Learning

The Digital Dreamers initiative is designed to provide a comprehensive experience for both students and teachers. The program includes both technical and non-technical elements to support the growth and development of both parties. The goal is to enable students and teachers to utilize and hone all their talents and skills, going beyond the traditional classroom setting.

Overall, this initiative aims to help young people develop the skills and confidence they need to succeed in life and work, regardless of their background or circumstances.

For more information regarding this initiative, please contact us at info@vidoori.com.
