Who We Are

We are solution focused and people driven.

Vidoori is a consulting firm that provides high quality information technology services and products to solve business problems for Government and Commercial clients. Our approach uses an unyielding focus on quality delivery and the innovative use of industry best practices.

A Trusted Partner

Our mission

To become your trusted partner in navigating the fast changing technology world through consulting, software, and services.

We aim to provide business and government clients with a measurable return on investment. We have years of demonstrated success delivering projects on time and according to expectation. Our team's experience spans numerous sectors across the federal government, healthcare, commercial, and non-profit organizations.
