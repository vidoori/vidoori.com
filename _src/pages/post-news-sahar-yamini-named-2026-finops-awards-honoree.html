Sahar Yamini Named a 2026 FinOps Awards Honoree

October 19, 2026 News

Vidoori is proud to announce that Sahar Yamini, Vice President, Enterprise Transformation & Applied AI, has been named a 2026 FinOps Awards Honoree. The honorees will be recognized at the Federal FinOps Summit in Washington, D.C., on November 4, 2026.

Several of our federal customers are being recognized this year as well, including the U.S. Census Bureau and the Department of Veterans Affairs, for their work in cloud optimization, financial accountability, and modernization.

FinOps is the discipline of managing cloud spending with the same rigor as any other part of operations: making costs visible, holding teams accountable for them, and making sure every dollar supports the mission. Vidoori works alongside federal agencies to modernize complex systems, optimize cloud operations, and deliver technology that has a measurable effect on the mission.

Congratulations to Sahar, to our federal partners, and to all of this year’s honorees. We are proud to be part of the work that is moving federal IT forward.
