Trong Khuong Bui

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Trong Bui founded Vidoori in 2008 with the vision of establishing an organization that embodies quality services, innovation, and a top-rated customer experience.

Prior to founding Vidoori, Trong had over 20 years of experience in engineering management spanning multiple large-scale federal and international agency programs, including leading the development and implementation of several innovative products and various large-scale technology transformation efforts.

Trong has an extensive track record with census projects domestically and internationally, providing technical leadership for census efforts in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.

Trong earned a B.S. in Computer Science from Morgan State University and an M.S. in Telecommunication and Networking from Johns Hopkins University.

EH

Eric Huang

Chief Strategy Officer

As Chief Strategy Officer, Eric Huang develops and oversees the overall strategic direction of the organization and strategic client programs, working closely with fellow executives to align strategy with business objectives.

Prior to joining Vidoori, Eric supported defense efforts for Northrop Grumman, where he held several management roles, and served in various engineering roles during his tenure at Lockheed Martin. Eric has played a vital role in the company's entry into the defense industry, including the recruitment of former officers and intelligence experts.

Eric holds a B.S. in Information Systems Management from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County.
