Certifications

Independent appraisal and certification of how we deliver, manage quality, and account for cost.

CMMI® V3.0 — Maturity Level 3

Appraised forDevelopment & Services

Vidoori has been appraised at CMMI® Maturity Level 3 for both Development and Services under the latest CMMI Version 3.0 model. This recognition reflects our organization's ability to consistently deliver high quality software and IT services through well-defined, repeatable, and continuously improving processes.

View the published appraisal

ISO 9001:2015 and ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018

Certified byNSF

Vidoori's Quality Management System is certified by NSF to meet ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 20000-1:2018 standards. This certification demonstrates Vidoori's commitment to meet and exceed industry standards for quality management.

DCAA Compliance

Accounting systemAudited

Vidoori has an accounting system that is compliant with Defense Contract Audit Agency (DCAA) requirements. Vidoori's accounting practices and procedures were subject to a DCAA audit and were found compliant with federal laws and regulations, particularly as they apply to the tracking and monitoring of costs.
