Largest Cybersecurity Companies List for 2021

October 22, 2021 Cybersecurity News

Vidoori is pleased to be recognized as #13 on the Washington Business Journal’s list of Largest Cybersecurity Companies in Greater Washington, D.C. This ranking is based on 2020 metro-area revenue.

Vidoori’s cybersecurity capabilities provide immense value to both the Federal and Department of Defense (DoD) sectors. We continue to augment our cyber practice to include cloud security, artificial intelligence, and advanced data security.

As an agile organization, Vidoori creates bespoke security solutions that meet the needs of our customers. This includes developing offensive and defensive approaches to protecting data from theft and damage. We also focus on hiring the very best talent and applying their expertise to the most challenging problem sets within the cyber ecosystem. Most recently, we utilized this talent to identify a critical cyber related need within the Department of Defense and created an artificial intelligence (AI) platform to support the unique requirements.

Therefore, it is an honor to be recognized by the Washington Business Journal as one of the largest cybersecurity firms in the Washington, D.C. area. We are pleased that our commitment in this area is recognized, and we will continue to play our role in securing the data and infrastructure of our clients.

The list of Largest Cybersecurity Companies in Greater D.C. is available in the October 8, 2021 issue of The Washington Business Journal.
