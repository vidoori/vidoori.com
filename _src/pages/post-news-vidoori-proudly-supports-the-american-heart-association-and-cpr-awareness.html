Proud Supporter of the D.C. Heart Ball

April 22, 2022 News

SILVER SPRING, MD. — (April 22, 2022) — For nearly 100 years, the American Heart Association (AHA) has been fighting heart disease and stroke while helping families and communities thrive. This year Vidoori is a proud supporter of the AHA Greater Washington Region Heart Ball, focused on raising critical funds for cardiovascular research and increasing awareness of lifesaving CPR skills.

A core component of Vidoori’s culture has always been the emphasis on health and well-being of our team and community,” said Trong Khuong Bui, Founder & Chief Executive Officer of Vidoori. “We are honored to be a supporter of an organization and event with a positive impact on the community and those affected by heart disease.

Trong Khuong Bui, Founder & Chief Executive Officer of Vidoori

We’re coming together on April 23 to celebrate the rhythm of our lives and recognize the power each of us has to be a lifesaver. Join us for an exquisite evening featuring fine dining, dancing, exclusive live and silent auctions, special guests and a tribute to our 2022 Faces of Heart, Bryan and Chris.

Each year, 350,000 out-of-hospital cardiac arrests occur in the United States, and in DC, only 5% of victims survive. Bystander CPR and early access to emergency medical services provide the foundation for a better long-term prognosis, improve patient outcomes and are critical for successful resuscitation. Knowing and performing CPR when a cardiac arrest occurs more than doubles a person’s chances of survival. That’s why our 2022 Faces of Heart, Bryan and Chris, are sharing their story illustrating the widespread impact CPR has on a person, family and community.

The impact you make by taking part in the Heart Ball is felt beyond the ballroom because it supports our community initiatives. We are thankful for sponsors like Vidoori who bring our events to life and lead the charge to educate, inspire and connect people across our region in the mission of the American Heart Association.

Soula Antoniou, Executive Director of the AHA, Greater Washington Region

To reserve a ticket or learn more, please visit dcheartball.heart.org or email Anita.Walker@Heart.org.
