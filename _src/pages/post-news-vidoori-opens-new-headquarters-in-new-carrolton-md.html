Vidoori Opens New Maryland Headquarters

November 7, 2022 News

Hyattsville, MD, November 7, 2022 – Vidoori, an IT consulting firm, is pleased to announce the official opening of its new headquarter location at 4000 Garden City Drive, Hyattsville, MD 20785. The new 8th floor location has been designed to prominently feature Vidoori’s brand and core values.

A lot of effort was put into ensuring that the office design and location fit the essence of the Vidoori brand and where we are heading for the future

Trong Khuong Bui, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Vidoori

The location features state of the art gym facilities, metro accessibility, and is ADA compliant. Vidoori is holding an internal team grand opening on November 7th as the office is officially open for all employees. In the future, Vidoori will also host open house events with clients, partners, and the business community. The headquarters serves at the central point for project management and houses the Vidoori Innovation Lab.
