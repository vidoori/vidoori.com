Vidoori Joins Team Selected for New Contract

June 18, 2021 News

Silver Spring, MD, June 10, 2021 – Vidoori Inc., a growing technology leader delivering integration & testing, cybersecurity, and data solutions to federal customers, is part of a team that has secured a contract for Enterprise Database Services with the U.S. Census Bureau. The single award blanket purchase agreement (BPA) is a $82 million contract for five (5) years that will support the modernization of database services and establish an Enterprise Database Center of Excellence (DCoE). The BPA is primed by Imagine Believe Realize (IBR) LLC, a partner that Vidoori worked with closely in pursuit of this opportunity.

This contract marks the second Center of Excellence that Vidoori supports for the U.S. Census Bureau. Vidoori also supports the management and marketing of the Test Center of Excellence (TCoE). The TCoE supports various sized software quality and integration testing efforts across the Census enterprise. This experience will be leveraged to support the successful establishment of the DCoE. The DCoE will support the Census’ mission to serve as the nation’s leading provider of quality data about its people and economy.
