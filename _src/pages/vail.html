Inside VAIL

Learn by building. Innovate with purpose.

The Vidoori Applied Innovation Lab is Vidoori’s internal research and development and applied-AI lab.

College graduates work alongside senior staff to learn Vidoori’s engineering environment, software development lifecycle (SDLC), and tools. Through research and hands-on product development, they help address government customer needs and prepare to transition into engineering positions on government contracts.
