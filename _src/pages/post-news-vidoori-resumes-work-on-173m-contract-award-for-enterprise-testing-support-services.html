Vidoori Resumes Work on $173M Contract Award for Enterprise Testing Support Services

October 13, 2023 News

In 2022, Vidoori won a five-year, $173 million Single Award Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) to provide Enterprise Testing Support Services for the U.S. Census Bureau (USCB). However, the award was protested, which caused all activities to be put on hold. Vidoori is excited to announce that the U.S. Court of Federal Claims ruled in Vidoori’s favor, denying all of the grounds for the protest, allowing Vidoori to resume providing services under the BPA. As of late August 2023, Vidoori resumed its focus on delivering enterprise-wide testing support services for the USCB.

Since 2015, Vidoori has been a trusted partner of the USCB, supporting several projects across the Census Bureau. Notably, this included establishing the Bureau’s first formal enterprise-wide testing practice. The recently secured contract allows Vidoori to continue supporting the Census Bureau’s mission by testing the quality and integration of the numerous systems that power data-driven activities. Vidoori’s extensive USCB experience and integration and testing expertise will ensure that the USCB’s suite of internal and public platforms function optimally and align with the Bureau’s rigorous quality criteria.

The USCB’s mission is to serve as the nation’s leading provider of quality demographic and economic data. This mission requires the Census Bureau to ensure that its systems garner trust and are not rendered inoperable due to defects. In support of that mission, Vidoori expects to implement innovations that support testing efficiency while ensuring data security and privacy. This includes utilizing Vidoori’s expertise in synthetic data generation, which allows robust testing of production systems without exposing sensitive data.

“We are honored to continue supporting the Bureau’s mission.”- Eric Huang, Chief Strategy Officer. “We are especially excited about the opportunity to combine our extensive Census experience with innovative solutions for enterprise testing. We truly believe they will help the Bureau as it progresses toward future goals.”

By leveraging our comprehensive integration and testing capabilities, The USCB will have greater confidence in its software systems’ reliability, security, and performance. This, in turn, enhances the Bureau’s ability to service citizens, support businesses, and uphold the critical data collection and reporting functions it performs for the country.

Vidoori is excited to embark on this collaboration with the USCB, and we look forward to helping the USCB achieve its goals of ensuring the highest quality software solutions support its mission on behalf of the United States.
