Vidoori Awarded Contract with NAVSUP

October 25, 2021 News Test & Integration

SILVER SPRING, MD — (BUSINESS WIRE) — Vidoori, a growing technology leader delivering testing and integration, cyber security and data solutions to federal customers, has recently been awarded a contract to provide Oracle Database support for the Navy Supply Systems Command’s Business Systems Center. The contract win is an extension of existing work between Vidoori and NAVSUP and runs through September 2022.

The award will enable Vidoori to continue supporting NAVSUP’s Oracle Database functions and includes senior and mid-level staffing support requisite experience, knowledge and certifications.

Vidoori has been providing support to NAVSUP since 2016 in the areas of Department of Defense Risk Management Framework, Oracle Database administration and video and collaborative systems Risk Management Framework documentation and testing.

Building upon our work with NAVSUP over the last five years is a testimony to the value we bring to partners in the Department of Defense, and ultimately the Warfighter, said Vishal Dua, Senior Vice President of Technology and Innovation at Vidoori. We take great pride in being able to provide expertise and solutions for critical information technology that ultimately supports mission success.

Vidoori’s customer familiarity, resources and expertise enables quick integration and support of the NAVSUP Business Systems Center’s mission as the Navy’s information technology provider responsible for the design, development and maintenance of information systems supporting logistics, supply chain management, transportation, finance and accounting.
