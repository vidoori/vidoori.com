External Referral Program

Refer and earn up to $2,000

Great people know great people. Refer someone to Vidoori — if we hire them, you could earn $2,000 through our External Referral Program.

Submit a referral

Program rules

To participate you must be an individual (not a business entity) and a legal resident of the United States who is at least 18 years of age.

Review the list of eligible jobs before referring, and confirm that both you as the referrer and your candidate meet the program rules.

To ensure your referral is considered for the most appropriate position, please provide a resume and job title.

Current Vidoori employees should continue to refer through the internal Employee Referral Program, not this one.
