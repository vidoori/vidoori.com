GSA MAS

Multiple Award Schedule

The GSA Schedule, also known as the Federal Supply Schedule and Multiple Award Schedule (MAS), is a long-term governmentwide contract with commercial companies that provides access to millions of commercial products and services at fair and reasonable prices. MAS makes buying efficient by connecting government buyers and industry.

GSA 8(a) STARS III

Best-in-Class small business set-aside GWAC

GSA 8(a) STARS III is a Best-in-Class small business set-aside Governmentwide Acquisition Contract that provides federal agencies with innovative information technology services. In addition to the general IT services scope, Vidoori's award also includes the sub-area of Emerging Technologies.

SeaPort NxG

Department of the Navy

SeaPort NxG was established to provide the Navy with proven contractors who can deliver services spanning the mission areas and technical capabilities supported by Department of the Navy ordering activities. Services fall within two categories — Engineering Services and Program Management Services — across 23 functional area subcategories.
