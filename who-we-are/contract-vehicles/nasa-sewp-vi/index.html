About the vehicle

About NASA SEWP VI

Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) VI is a multiple-award Government-Wide Acquisition Contract managed by the NASA SEWP Program Office.

It gives federal agencies a streamlined route to information technology, communications, and audio/visual products, solutions, and services. Vidoori holds Category C — ITC/AV Mission-Based Services.

Ordering

How to order through SEWP VI

Federal customers order through the NASA SEWP Quote Request Tool. Vidoori quotes only offerings on contract in the SEWP database of record, at or below contract price.

  1. Step 01

    Prepare your requirement

    Define the services, performance work statement or statement of work, period of performance, evaluation criteria, and response date.

  2. Step 02

    Submit through the SEWP QRT

    Select Category C, your NAICS code, and business size designation. Requests route to eligible contract holders under FAR 16.505(b).

  3. Step 03

    Evaluate quotes

    Evaluate responses against the criteria stated in your request and document the selection according to your agency procedures.

  4. Step 04

    Place the order

    Route the delivery order through the SEWP Program Office, referencing Vidoori’s contract number and the selected quote.

Purchase cards are accepted. Vidoori does not quote or invoice any SEWP administrative charge as a separate line; it is embedded in offering prices by contract.
