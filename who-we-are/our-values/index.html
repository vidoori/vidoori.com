Who We Are/Our Values

Values drive our commitment.

Our team understands that the combination of process efficiency and customer-oriented values drives the success of any project. We value teamwork and developing strong connections with our clients.

The Best People

To deliver value to our clients we need a team that operates at a high level. Vidoori takes a proactive approach to attracting, developing, and retaining top talent. We look for individuals who appreciate a challenging environment and are driven toward creating value for clients.

Ethics and Integrity

At the foundation of our business culture is a desire to do the right thing. This applies to how we do business with our clients and how we work with each other. We take pride in our work and value transparency internally and with our clients.

Innovation

Innovation lets us find improved ways to deliver value. Vidoori places continuous focus on how we deliver and how we operate, and we proactively look for opportunities to develop new technologies and inspire our team to keep learning.

Collaboration

Vidoori values a team-oriented environment and believes that input from a diverse team yields great results. We understand that an inclusive, engaging approach yields better client relationships.

Value and Quality

We place an unyielding focus on delivering value to our clients, aiming to create long term trusting relationships. Our approach weaves high quality delivery into the services and products that support our clients' missions.

Support the Greater Good

Vidoori has made a conscious commitment to support activities that benefit individual and environmental well-being in our local and global community. We provide monetary and fundraising support to activities that support education, improved well-being, and a healthy environment.

Diversity and Inclusion

True excellence requires a commitment to equity, respect, dignity, and acceptance. At Vidoori we believe our differences make us stronger, producing an environment of mutual respect and acceptance without biases. We strive to listen to our team to maintain an environment that respects diversity of traditions and experiences.

Health, Safety and Well-Being

Our team values and expects a safe and healthy work environment. We strive to maintain an environment of not only physical health and safety but also mental and emotional well-being, including virtual, confidential health and wellness programs.

Values you would recognize?

We are always interested in people who want to work this way.
