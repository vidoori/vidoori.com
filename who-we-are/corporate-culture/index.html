Who We Are/Corporate Culture

Everyone adds value. Each voice is heard.

We take pride in our collaborative and supportive culture, where professionals from different backgrounds and career paths work together toward a common goal.

Vidoori is a very diverse family where everyone adds value and each voice is heard. At Vidoori we strive to put our people first and support them in their career growth.

In order to maintain a positive culture and provide the best service to our customers, Vidoori focuses on hiring and retaining the brightest talent. Employees are our most valuable asset, and we are continuously looking for ways to make them feel part of our growing family.

Work environment

Vidoori promotes several initiatives to foster a great work environment and allow team bonding. Our monthly engagement activities help employees from different teams get to know each other away from their desks, while our quarterly All-Hands meetings share and celebrate corporate successes in a collaborative setting.

Family friendly events

Our biannual corporate events bring employees and their families together, in recognition of the support that families provide — a big part of our employees' success.

Wellness

Our recurring fitness challenges are designed to promote physical and mental wellness among our employees.

Career growth

We look for individuals who appreciate a challenging environment and are driven toward creating value for clients, and we back that with learning opportunities and internal mobility.
