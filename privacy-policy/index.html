Privacy Policy

Introduction

This privacy notice has been compiled to better serve those who are concerned with how their ‘Personally Identifiable Information’ (PII) is being used online. PII, as used in US privacy law and information security, is information that can be used on its own or with other information to identify, contact, or locate a single person, or to identify an individual in context. Please read our privacy policy carefully to gain a clear understanding of how Vidoori intends to collect, use, protect, and handle the user's PII in accordance with Vidoori's website.

Vidoori's commitment to user privacy

At Vidoori your privacy is important to us. We are committed to complying with all applicable federal, state, local, and international privacy laws and regulations. We provide this privacy notice as part of our commitment to privacy by describing what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and with whom we share it.

What information is collected and how Vidoori uses it

While visiting our website, the user may be asked to voluntarily provide personal information in order to accomplish the purpose of their visit. For example, if the user is looking for a career opportunity with Vidoori and follows a career link, they may be asked to provide their email address, residence, education, work experience, and other personal information typically found on a résumé. Vidoori will then use that information to evaluate the user's qualifications for the inquired position.

If the user submits our contact form, we collect the name, email address, telephone number, organization, region, and message content that the user provides, and use it solely to route and respond to that inquiry.

Precautions taken to protect user privacy

Vidoori uses industry standard security practices, controls, and procedures to safeguard any PII that is provided by individual users against loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.

Cookies and other technical considerations

Vidoori does not use cookies for tracking purposes.

Our contact form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, an anti-abuse service that helps us distinguish human visitors from automated ones. Turnstile may store information in the user's browser and process technical signals, including IP address, strictly for the purpose of that abuse prevention.

The user may choose to have their computer warn them each time a cookie is being sent, or choose to disable all cookies, through their preferred browser's settings. Each browser is different, so the browser's ‘Help’ menu may assist the user in learning the correct way to modify cookies.

Please note that if the user disables cookies, some features may be disabled, which may make the website experience less efficient, and some of the website's features may not function properly.

Third party access to user information

Vidoori does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer personally identifiable information to outside parties. Occasionally, at our discretion, Vidoori may include or offer third-party products or services on our website. These third-party sites have separate and independent privacy policies. Therefore, Vidoori has no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites. Nonetheless, Vidoori seeks to protect the integrity of our site and welcomes any feedback about these sites.

Children's privacy

Vidoori's website is not designed with the intent to collect information from children under the age of 13. We ask that children under the age of 13 not submit any personal information to Vidoori while viewing our website. If the user believes that Vidoori has collected information regarding a child under 13, please contact us using the contact information below so that we may take proper action, including deleting the information.

Updates to Vidoori's privacy notice

Vidoori may update this privacy notice periodically without prior notice to reflect changes in our practice. If a user has volunteered personal information to Vidoori and would like to update it, please use the contact information below to provide us with the updated information.
