Software Development

Innovative software solutions that achieve a high rate of user adoption.

A Proven Approach

Users engaged from the start

Vidoori develops custom applications for mobile, web, or stand-alone use on open source and Microsoft platforms — for commercial clients, non-profits, multi-national organizations, and the U.S. Federal Government.

We engage key users and stakeholders early in the development process through agile methodologies, clickable prototypes, fast feedback loops, and automation. Across a wide span of cross-industry projects we have developed real know-how in delivering modern applications that support business processes and reduce cost.

Application Modernization

Low-risk, incremental modernization

As technology advances, legacy applications become incompatible with newer capabilities like cloud, mobile, and analytics. This limits your organization's ability to benefit from technology advances and hinders digital transformation.

We help modernize legacy applications using an incremental, low-risk approach. This includes moving legacy architecture to cloud-native and modernizing user interfaces.
