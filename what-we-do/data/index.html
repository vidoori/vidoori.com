What We Do/Data Management

Data Management

Data is the foundation for intelligent reporting and insights. Our capabilities let you use data to drive business decisions — including how to apply integrated data sets to yield results that drive real outcomes.

From data collection through analysis and real-time display, we have leveraged cloud and internally hosted services to give organizations insight into their operations and to identify risks and bottlenecks. Our solutions integrate information from multiple data sources to display real-time metrics for organizational, government, and civilian activities.

Our data management capabilities cover data integration, data quality assurance, data warehousing, and data governance. Whether you are consolidating data from multiple sources, improving the accuracy and consistency of your data, or implementing a robust data governance framework, our team has the expertise to help you achieve your goals.

Our data scientists work closely with clients to understand their goals and develop customized solutions. We have extensive experience across government, finance, healthcare, and technology.

Synthetic Data Generation

A secure alternative to real data

Organizations need large volumes of data to train machine-learning models, validate algorithms, and drive innovation. But collecting and storing real data can be expensive, time-consuming, and raise privacy and security concerns.

Our team uses advanced algorithms and machine-learning techniques to generate realistic, customizable synthetic data that mimics real-world patterns and behaviors.

Our synthetic data is designed to meet your needs, from small sample sizes to large-scale datasets — whether you are training a model, conducting research, generating test data at volume, or performing analytics.

We understand the importance of data privacy and take this responsibility seriously. Synthetic data is generated in a secure, controlled environment so that sensitive information is protected.
