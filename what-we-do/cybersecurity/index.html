What We Do/Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity

Vidoori helps clients prevent cyber incidents that lead to loss of trust and loss of clients.

Proven Expertise

Anticipate threats, don't just react

Our team of certified cybersecurity professionals helps commercial and federal clients develop offensive and defensive threat protection strategies — so clients can anticipate threats in advance rather than only respond to them.

Our professionals help ensure your organization is positioned to effectively mitigate advanced persistent threat (APT) activity within your network or that of your customers. We can also help right-size your Information Assurance (IA) compliance measures, protecting your agency or company from loss due to improperly implemented government-required security controls.

A robustly implemented cybersecurity program increases company effectiveness while reducing costs associated with bad actor outcomes such as network penetration and loss of intellectual property.

Right-sized solutions

We deliver the cybersecurity expertise that enables your organization to execute projects, products, and initiatives on time and on budget. Throughout, we employ transparent strategy and reporting so you have the information needed to make sound decisions.

How we work

Vidoori maintains a network of alliances with many vendors in the cybersecurity market, giving us access to a wide array of technologies. Our professionals are actively engaged in industry associations and communities of practice.

An expanded network

Our network of vendors, communities, and industry associations supports our ability to deliver comprehensive, tailored cybersecurity solutions that meet the specific needs of each client.
